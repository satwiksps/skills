# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Assume good intent while addressing observable behavior. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving technically grounded feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and disclosure boundaries;
- helping newcomers reproduce and understand a problem.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close interactions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and consistent.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact the maintainer through the private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include private personal information in a public issue.
